Rainbow Obby — a cute, simple 15-stage obstacle course for Roblox. Jump across rainbow platforms, dodge spinning kill bricks, ride moving platforms, cross disappearing pads, and reach the gold WIN pad at stage 15. Checkpoints save your stage, the leaderboard tracks Stage and Wins, and the UI is all pastel, rounded and friendly — Fredoka One titles, Gotham Bold buttons, clouds, balloons and a rainbow in the sky. Free to play, no Robux, no paid features.

## Run it

```sh
Open the place in Roblox Studio and press Play
```
